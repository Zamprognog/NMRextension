## files:

* yago-taxonomy: from yago4.5 release, taxonomy of classes
* yago-schema: from yago4.5 release, contains shacl constraints
* yago-facts-nolit: from yago-facts of yago4.5 release, filtered to remove al literals (out of scope of LP)
* yago_4.5_triples.nt , yago_4.5_triples.txt: filtered version of yago-facts-nolit where non LP appropriate triples are
  filtered out 
* yago4.5-10.nt, yago4.5-10.tsv: full dataset of yago4.5-10: similarly to yago3-10, it contains all LP-appropriate triples that contain entities appearing in at least 10 triples in the dataset (i.e. dense enough)
